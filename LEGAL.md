# Legal & Compliance Checklist

*DISCLAIMER: This document is not a substitute for formal Nigerian legal counsel. It provides a baseline checklist for launching the NACOS 100 platform.*

## Compliance Areas
- **NDPA (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)**:
  - Is registration required due to volume (>1000 users)? *LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED*
  - Has a Privacy Notice been created detailing lawful basis, purpose limitation, data minimization, and retention?
  - Are cross-border processing considerations addressed (if Supabase is hosted outside Nigeria)?
- **Consumer Protection (FCCPC)**:
  - Clear terms regarding returns, refunds (especially for cash payments), and non-delivery.
  - Transparent product descriptions and accurate pricing.
- **Contractual & Marketplace Rules**:
  - **Coin Terms**: Coins are promotional credits with no cash equivalent.
  - **Seller Terms**: Approved sellers must conform to marketplace rules (no prohibited goods, strict fulfillment).
  - Liability limitations must be legally sound, not blanket waivers violating FCCPC guidelines.
  
## Pre-Launch Actions
